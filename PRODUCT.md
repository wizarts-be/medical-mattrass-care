## Platform

## Stack

Astro, static output. Must stay convertible to a WordPress block theme later (wp-builder-agent). Three.js and GSAP allowed for the home experience.

## What it is

Website for **Medical Mattress Care BV** (MMC), a Flemish manufacturer (since 2005, founder and manager Michel Marynissen) of hygienic, pressure-relieving mattresses, positioning cushions, pillows, duvets and bed linen for care homes, hospitals and other care institutions. Products are developed with research institutions. Private customers can order but are not the target.

## Users and job

B2B buyers at care institutions: purchasing, management, head nurses, occupational therapists, technical services. They look first by **product**, then **price/quality**, then **delivery time**. They want sizes, composition, density and standards, quickly.

## Primary action

Request a quote / price (offerte). Secondary: request a sample, call. No webshop, no prices on the site.

## Positioning (client's words)

Everything deliverable from stock with fast service, sharp prices, the highest quality on the market. Custom sizes and builds possible. Hero asset: the **MMC 210 PU cover** (MRSA-resistant, washable at 95 °C, sterilisable to 115 °C, CRIB 5, Oeko-Tex 100), standard on mattresses and most positioning aids.

Competitors: Duvatex, Sampli, Euromousse.

## Featured products

MMC V10 mattress, MMC PUP 23 pillow, the duvets (MMC Bicolor), the seat cushions (R3DV 07, ZP 05). Only active products; products struck through in the corrected catalogue are out.

Search terms: medische matras, visco matras, incontinentie matras, decubitus matras, drukverdelende matrassen, plus the featured products.
