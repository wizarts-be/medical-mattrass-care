# Surface: whole site (home, producten, categorie, product, inner pages)

Mode: Persuade (home, categorie, product), Operate-light (producten finder, offerte form).
Audience: purchasers of care institutions; job: find product by need or code, then request a quote. Proof: order codes, sizes, densities, norms, stock from Sint-Pauwels. Constraint: no invented counts, clients, delivery times; fabric hero kept (user).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, polished B2B catalogue: a purchaser finds the product, its code, size and stock status, and asks for a quote. Refuses decorative metaphors and gimmicks (user restart after "Stock Rack").

OWN-WORLD: Near-white paper, light grey photo panels, Fira Sans 400/500, Fira Mono codes, MMC indigo for actions, the offerte band and the footer; orange only in the logo; soft 6 to 16px radii; thin rules.

STORY: MMC is a reliable Flemish supplier with everything in stock; I find my product fast and request a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "Zorgmatrassen en hulpstukken, uit stock geleverd", one sentence, Offerte aanvragen and Bekijk het assortiment, three checks; the fabric photo right in a rounded panel with a card linking to the MMC V10.

FORM: Calm B2B catalogue (user-directed restart; no seed roll).
