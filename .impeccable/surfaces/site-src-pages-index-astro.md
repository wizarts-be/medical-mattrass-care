# Surface: home (src/pages/index.astro) and site shell

Mode: Persuade (home). Inner product pages: Operate/Read inside the same world.
Audience: purchasers, head nurses, OTs and technical services at Flemish care institutions. Action: Offerte aanvragen. Proof: certificates only.
Memorable moment: pressing into the 3D mattress and watching it slowly recover, then the bed coming apart layer by layer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a care bed being made and unmade, seen from above: every layer (sheet, 210 PU cover, visco, CMHR) folds back to show what it does. Refuses the category default of white page, smiling-nurse stock photo and a tile grid of products.

OWN-WORLD: (Steered by the user on 2026-11-09: calmer, lighter, more white space, full-bleed sections.) White and cool-sheet grounds own most of the page; indigo PU (#352C57) lives in the 3D mattress and in one full-bleed band; orange (#E68626) is the primary action, the pressure heat and the closing field. Sections run edge to edge (split cover section, category tile grid, certificate strip). No gauges or dashboard metaphors. Bold (650–700), large, left-aligned Archivo display with asymmetric, staggered and overlapping layouts (bento range, offset statements, a detail image breaking a band); Fira Sans for text. Square corners, hospital-corner fold, zipper rule in spec tables.

STORY: In one screen the buyer sees "medical mattresses and aids for care, from stock, custom, Flemish" and a quote button. Scrolling, they see the V10 built layer by layer, the cover's six proofs as instruments, the range as an index, why MMC, certificates, and they request a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: (Steered again 2026-11-09.) Full-bleed photo of the indigo PU fabric under a solid white header; huge bold left-aligned headline 'Minder druk. / Meer hygiëne.' (white + orange) bottom-left, sub and CTA pair in a second column bottom-right; a small white card top-right links down to the MMC V10. The 3D press-and-unmake mattress lives in the next section (left-aligned sticky heading), with a rendered still + layer list when WebGL2 is unavailable.

FORM: The made bed, own grounded list position 7, seed key e3fba6e2. Raises: six-pack gauges for cover specs; text banded per layer thickness; fading imprint as state everywhere; flat full-field colour commitment; numbered fold sequence for maatwerk.
