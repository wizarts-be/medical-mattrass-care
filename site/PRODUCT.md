# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: purchasers and management (aankopers, directie) of woonzorgcentra, hospitals and other care institutions in Flanders. They search by product first, then compare price and quality, then delivery time, and want a quote fast. Secondary: head nurses, ergotherapists and technical services who choose the right product for a resident's problem. Private buyers can order but get no attention on the site.

## Product Purpose

The website of Medical Mattress Care BV: hygienic, pressure-relieving care mattresses, positioning aids, cushions, duvets, sheets and covers. B2B, no webshop, no prices. Success is a quote request (Offerte aanvragen), secondarily a sample or a technical sheet request, or a phone call. Today clients come by word of mouth; the site must convince professionally and be findable on: medische matras, visco matras, incontinentie matras, decubitus matras, drukverdelende matrassen.

## Positioning

Leads with stock and fast service: nearly every standard product is delivered from stock in Sint-Pauwels, at a sharp price for top quality thanks to a low cost structure. Supported by the MMC 210 PU-hoes (MRSA-resistant, washable at 95 °C, sterilisable to 115 °C, CRIB 5, Oeko-Tex 100) standard on every mattress and aid, custom work in build-up and size, and a personal family business founded in 2005 by Michel Marynissen. Competitors: Duvatex, Sampli, Euromousse.

## Operating Context

Purchasers compare suppliers with order codes (MMC V10 A, MMCP AK 01A), sizes in cm, foam densities (CMHR 40, Visco 50) and norms. They request quotes for quantities per ward. The MMC Thuis matras has CNK codes for the pharmacy channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

## Brand Commitments

- Logo SVGs (indigo matras icon, orange MMC wordmark, baseline "Medical Mattress Care BV"); never redrawn.
- Fira Sans, MMC indigo #352C57, orange #E68626 (never white text on orange). Soft radii (6 to 16 px) in the v2 calm design. Wizarts asked (latest): a polished, modern, calm B2B site with clear information and no gimmicks.
- Voice: zakelijk, factual, warm; "u"; "de matras"; units with a space; exclamation marks only on stickers; no em dashes.

- Absent, never to be invented: client names or logos, testimonials, counts of institutions or mattresses, delivery-time figures, prices.

## Product Principles

1. Product first: every screen leads a purchaser to a concrete product, its code, and a quote.
2. Facts over adjectives: sizes, densities, norms, order codes.
3. Stock and speed are the promise; show them without inventing numbers.
4. Personal, not corporate: Michel and Sint-Pauwels are real.
