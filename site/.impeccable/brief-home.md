# Surface: whole site (home, producten, categorie, product, inner pages)

Mode: Persuade (home, categorie, product), Operate-light (producten finder, offerte form).
Audience: purchasers of care institutions; job: find product by need or code, then request a quote. Proof: order codes, sizes, densities, norms, stock from Sint-Pauwels. Constraint: no invented counts, clients, delivery times; fabric hero kept (user).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the MMC depot in Sint-Pauwels. Products stand on indigo rack beams with white shelf labels (order code, name, size); visitors walk aisles per care need and pick products onto a pick list that becomes the quote. Refuses the category default: hero, category tiles, USP row, CTA band.

OWN-WORLD: Cool concrete-grey ground, indigo #352C57 uprights and orange #E68626 beams (the colours of real pallet racking, and the MMC brand pair), white shelf labels with Fira Mono code, name and size. Orange is reserved for the beams that carry stock and for action (pick list, quote button); the stock claim is a white pallet sticker with an indigo rim, never coloured text. Amended after the finish review: beams orange instead of indigo, because pallet racking is indigo/blue uprights with orange beams. Square corners, no shadows, no cards; things stand on beams.

STORY: MMC has it in stock; I find my aisle, see the product on its shelf with its code, add it to my list, request the quote in one go.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed indigo fabric photo; headline "Zorgmatrassen uit stock" at poster scale low left; a full-width rack beam along the bottom carrying a shelf label for MMC V10 with an orange "Op voorraad" pallet sticker and the Offerte aanvragen action; round orange Maatwerk seal.

FORM: The Stock Rack, own list position 1 (user picked IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed 393b0021. Signature interaction: "Op mijn lijst" on every shelf label fills the pick list (nav counter, close section, prefilled offerte form).
